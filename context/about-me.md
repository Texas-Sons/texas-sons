# About Morgan Valdez

**Role:** Founder, Lead Designer & Engineer at **Texas Sons**.
**Location:** Pleasanton, Texas.
**Mission:** Provide Texas local businesses, political campaigns, and leaders with high-converting, modern, automated digital infrastructure and 1-click web platforms.

## Core Strengths & Working Style
- **Local-first relationships:** Approachable, community-grounded, respectful of local Texas leadership and small business dynamics.
- **Show-don't-tell execution:** Prefers presenting clients with working, live interactive demos before asking for commitments.
- **Automation-driven:** Focuses on building reusable component architecture, generative blueprints, and agentic workflows to eliminate repetitive manual coding.

## Top Operational Pains
- **Asset Gathering & Initial Customization:** Manual back-and-forth gathering client bios, headshots, and requirements.
- **Pre-QC Website Building:** Hand-crafting initial site variations before quality control checks and error audits.
- **Centralized Tracking:** Needing an integrated hub to manage client sprint tickets, credentials, lead notifications, and project status.
