# About Texas Sons

**Texas Sons** is an autonomous web design agency and digital platform builder engineered specifically for Texas local businesses, political campaigns, commercial trades, and community enterprises.

## The Offer
1. **1-Click Live Blueprints & Sites:** Generates and deploys bespoke, mobile-first websites on Cloudflare Pages with sub-second load times.
2. **Interactive Community & Conversion Tools:**
   - Political/Campaigns: Yard sign intake, volunteer registration, town hall event schedules, platform pillars, and compliant legal disclaimers.
   - Commercial Trades: 24/7 estimate requests, upfront service pricing cards, customer reviews.
   - Luxury Beauty & Food/Beverage: Appointment booking, digital menus, catering intake.
3. **Integrated Backend:** Real-time lead capture stored in Supabase, maps discovery via Google Maps Platform, and automated Stripe billing/invoicing.

## Target Audience (ICP)
- **Local Political Campaigns:** Judicial candidates, County Sheriffs, Commissioners, Mayoral/City Council candidates across Texas.
- **Home Services & Commercial Trades:** Electricians, HVAC, plumbers, roofers, general contractors.
- **Local Hospitality & Services:** Salons, spas, BBQ smokehouses, restaurants.

## Revenue & Operating Model
- Upfront setup + monthly recurring management/hosting retainers.
- Transactions and invoicing managed via Stripe, Bluevine, and Wave.
