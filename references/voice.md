# Voice & Communication Register — Morgan Valdez

Match this voice register when drafting emails, client proposals, outreach messages, and updates. Do not invent a corporate, overly academic, or fake tone.

## Golden Rules
1. **Local & Respectful:** Warm, respectful, personal greetings. Acknowledge local connections or common community ground naturally.
2. **Value & Demo First:** Never send a generic pitch without value. Lead with live preview links (`pages.dev` / portfolio references) and concrete benefits.
3. **Bulleted Clarity:** Use clean bullet points to summarize key features (e.g. Mobile-first speed, Platform pillars, Interactive intake tools, Branding & disclosures).
4. **Low-Friction Call-to-Action:** Propose a simple "quick 15-minute chat to go over demo and pricing" or direct reply.
