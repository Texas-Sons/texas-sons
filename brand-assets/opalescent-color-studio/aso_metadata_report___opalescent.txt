### Elevate Your Canvas.
Welcome to **Opalescent Color Studio**, San Antonio’s premier destination for high-end hair transformations. Our app brings the boutique salon experience directly to your fingertips, allowing you to explore our artistry and secure your next session with ease.

**Why Opalescent?**
At Opalescent, we believe hair is a canvas. Our expert colorists specialize in balayage, precision highlights, and custom color corrections designed to elevate your natural beauty.

**Key Features:**
*   **Seamless Booking:** View real-time availability and book appointments in seconds.
*   **Curated Portfolio:** Browse our gallery of signature transformations for inspiration.
*   **Transparent Pricing:** Explore our full menu of coloring, styling, and extension services.
*   **Artist Profiles:** Meet the experts behind the chair.
*   **Direct Access:** Quick links for directions, calls, and Instagram updates.

Experience the perfect blend of artistry and precision. Download the Opalescent app today and begin your journey toward a stunning new look. ✨
